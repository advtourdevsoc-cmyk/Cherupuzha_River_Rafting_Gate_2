💰 Amount Settings

Set the per-person amount for bookings based on the day of week.

₹
Default: 25%

Applied to Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri bookings

₹
Default: 35%

Applied to Saturday and Sunday bookings

%

Percentage fee added to the subtotal amount for each booking (e.g. enter 5 for 5%)

Info:
Subtotal = Group Size × Applicable Amount
Advance Payment = Subtotal × Advance %
Platform Fee Amount = Advance Payment × (Platform Fee % / 100)
Total amount for a booking = Subtotal + Platform Fee Amount

Example: Group of 4 on Friday with ₹25/person, 5% Platform Fee, and 25% advance:
Subtotal = 4 × ₹25 = ₹100
Advance Payment (25%) = ₹100 × 25% = ₹25
Platform Fee (5%) = ₹25 × 5% = ₹1.25
Total = ₹100 + ₹1.25 = ₹101.25

For Saturday and Sunday with ₹40/person, 5% Platform Fee, and 35% advance:
Subtotal = 4 × ₹40 = ₹160
Advance Payment (35%) = ₹160 × 35% = ₹56
Platform Fee (5%) = ₹56 × 5% = ₹2.80
Total = ₹160 + ₹2.80 = ₹162.80

Weekday Amount applies to Monday to Friday bookings.
Weekend Amount applies to Saturday and Sunday bookings.
